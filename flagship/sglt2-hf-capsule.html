Across the ejection-fraction spectrum, do SGLT2 inhibitors reduce cardiovascular death or worsening heart failure? An editable, self-auditing synthesis that re-runs as you change the data.

Protocol

Review question — PICO

PopulationAdults with heart failure across the full ejection-fraction spectrum (HFrEF, HFmrEF, HFpEF). InterventionAn SGLT2 inhibitor added to guideline therapy. ComparatorPlacebo. OutcomeComposite of cardiovascular death or worsening heart failure.

Eligibility & pre-registration

Randomized, placebo-controlled outcome trials reporting a hazard ratio for the primary composite. Living protocol v1.0. Effect measure: hazard ratio, pooled on the log scale, random-effects (DerSimonian–Laird) with a prediction interval.
