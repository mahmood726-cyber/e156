Ranking — with credible intervals, never bare ranks

Each treatment's odds ratio vs placebo with its 95% credible interval; SUCRA (surface under the cumulative ranking curve) shown at right. SUCRA alone can mislead — note how the credible intervals overlap, so a higher SUCRA does not prove superiority. Always read the intervals, not the rank.
